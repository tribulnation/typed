# Overview

Task-focused guides for the most common MEXC workflows.

## Included Guides

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md) - spot and futures market-data reads
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md) - place, query, open, cancel, and cancel all, for spot and futures
- [Fetch Balances, Positions & History](fetch-balances-positions-and-history.md) - spot balances, futures assets, positions, trades, and funding history
- [Manage Deposits & Withdrawals](manage-deposits-and-withdrawals.md) - deposit addresses, deposit/withdrawal history, submit and cancel a withdrawal
- [Listen To Streams](listen-to-streams.md) - spot and futures real-time subscriptions
