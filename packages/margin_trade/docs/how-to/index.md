# Overview

Task-focused guides for the most common client workflows.

## Included Guides

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md) - markets, order books, prices, candles and CoinGecko listings
- [Fetch Account Data](fetch-account-data.md) - balances, positions, fills, funding, transfers and performance
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md) - place, list, query, modify and cancel orders, and set leverage
- [Paginate Through Results](paginate-through-results.md) - walk fills, liquidations, announcements and referrals page by page
- [Listen To Live Streams](listen-to-live-streams.md) - order book, trades, prices and your own orders over WebSocket
- [Manage Deposits & Withdrawals](manage-deposits-withdrawals.md) - deposit addresses, withdrawals, perp/spot transfers and history
