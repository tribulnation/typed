# Overview

Task-focused guides for the Lighter client.

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md) - markets and decimals, order books, trades, candles, funding
- [Fetch Account Data](fetch-account-data.md) - balances, positions, open orders, order and trade history, PnL
- [Paginate Through Results](paginate-through-results.md) - cursor walks, time-range walks, resuming a walk
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md) - order types, scaled prices, modify, cancel, cancel-all, grouped orders
- [Listen To Streams](listen-to-streams.md) - public and private channels, snapshots and updates, dropped connections
- [Batch Transactions](batch-transactions.md) - reserve nonces, sign, and submit several transactions at once
- [Transfers & Sub-Accounts](transfers-and-sub-accounts.md) - sub-accounts, transfers, withdrawals, deposit history
- [Sign Offline](sign-offline.md) - sign transactions and auth tokens without sending them
