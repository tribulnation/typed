# Overview

Task-focused guides for the most common Bit2Me workflows.

## Included Guides

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md): tickers, order book, market config, candles, recent trades, and currency prices
- [Listen To Streams](listen-to-streams.md): public order book/trade streams, your own orders/trades/balance, and account notifications
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md): create, list, inspect, and cancel orders over REST or WebSocket
- [Fetch Account Data](fetch-account-data.md): trading balance, trade/funding history, wallet pockets, and Earn totals
- [Query & Manage Earn](manage-earn.md): Earn assets/yields, your Earn wallets, and depositing/withdrawing from Earn
- [Query & Manage Deposits/Withdrawals](manage-deposits-and-withdrawals.md): wallet pockets, deposit addresses, and moving funds
