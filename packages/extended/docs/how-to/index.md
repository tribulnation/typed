# Overview

Task-focused guides for the most common Extended workflows.

## Included Guides

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md) - markets, prices, order books, trades, candles, funding and open interest
- [Listen To Live Streams](listen-to-live-streams.md) - order book, trade and price streams, plus your account updates
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md) - signed limit orders with TP/SL, cancels, mass cancel and the dead man's switch
- [Fetch Account Data](fetch-account-data.md) - balances, positions, orders, trades, fees and leverage
- [Paginate Through Results](paginate-through-results.md) - walk trade, order, position and funding history
- [Transfer & Withdraw](transfer-and-withdraw.md) - move funds between sub-accounts and withdraw to Starknet or EVM chains
