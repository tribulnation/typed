# Overview

Task-focused guides for the most common GRVT workflows.

## Included Guides

- [Fetch Market Data](fetch-market-data.md) - instruments, tickers, order books, trades, candles and funding rates
- [Fetch Account Data](fetch-account-data.md) - account summaries, positions, fills and order history
- [Place & Manage Orders](place-and-manage-orders.md) - create, look up, list and cancel orders
- [Paginate Through Results](paginate-through-results.md) - walk history and candle endpoints with `_paged` methods
- [Listen To Live Streams](listen-to-live-streams.md) - public market feeds and your own order, fill and position updates
- [Manage Transfers & Withdrawals](manage-transfers-and-withdrawals.md) - deposit addresses, transfers, withdrawals and their history
